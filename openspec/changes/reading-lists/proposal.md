# Reading Lists — Proposal

## What

Introduce **reading lists**: named, ordered collections of series that span multiple libraries. A reading list is a curated sequence the user can name, reorder, and share via the client API.

## Why

- Users want to group series into thematic or sequential collections (e.g. "Saga SF à lire", "Intégrales Moebius")
- Series in a list may come from different libraries (BD FR / Manga)
- The client app needs to surface curated reading paths

## Out of scope

- Per-user lists (single global namespace for now)
- Books within a list (series only)
- List sharing / visibility flags
